My Story

Explorer at Heart

Geography captivated me from a young age. The drama of the Great Rift Valley, obsidian rock fields, volcanic mountains, dense forests, sweeping plateaus, and vast deserts — these landscapes make me feel alive. I love hiking and travelling to see new places and understand how the earth was shaped. Every trip is a lesson in nature's power.

Fascinated by Space

Astronomy is one of my deepest interests. The scale of the cosmos — the Milky Way, dark matter, black holes, the binary star system of Sirius A and B — fills me with wonder. I love asking the big questions: what is beyond what we can see? What is dark energy doing to the universe? Space humbles me and keeps me curious.

Grounded in History

Understanding the past gives me perspective on the present. I love studying historical events, ancient civilisations, and the decisions that changed the course of human life. History isn't just facts — it's the story of who we are and why the world looks the way it does today.

Driven by Business & Economics

I am passionate about how economies work — the role of government, trade, markets, and supply chains in everyday life. This is a big part of why I chose Supply Chain Management: it sits right at the intersection of economics and real-world problem solving. I want to build systems that make businesses run better and lives easier.
